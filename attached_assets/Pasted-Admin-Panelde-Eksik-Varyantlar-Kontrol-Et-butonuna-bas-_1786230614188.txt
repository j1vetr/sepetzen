Ürün Detay Sayfasında ki ürün fotoğrafı ve galeri kısmı için tasarım dilini iletiyorum aynısını yaparsın ilettiğim şekilde : 

DESKTOP
Aspect 3:4 portrait. zinc-900 background. rounded-xl.
Vertical thumbnail rail on the left (5 visible, scrolls if more).
Thumbnails: selected → 2px solid white ring with dark offset, others 50% opacity.
Main image: cursor-zoom-in, hover scales to 2x and tracks mouse position.
Click → fullscreen black lightbox.
Animated rotating dashed white SVG border around the main image frame.
Discount badge overlaid top-left (red, bold black text).

MOBILE
Thumbnail rail disappears.
Main image becomes a swipeable horizontal carousel (Embla, loop enabled).
Same 3:4 aspect, zinc-900, rounded-xl.
Thumbnails move below the image, centered, horizontal row.
If more than 5 images: small pagination dots appear under thumbnails
(selected dot wider and white, others white/30).
Tap image → fullscreen lightbox (swipe to navigate, no arrows).

LIGHTBOX (both)
Pure black fullscreen overlay.
Desktop: left/right chevron arrows, image max 90vw/90vh object-contain.
Mobile: full-screen swipe carousel, arrows hidden.
Bottom pagination dots. Click outside image → close.
